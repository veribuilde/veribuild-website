Privacy Policy (POPIA Compliance)

VeriBuild International is committed to protecting personal information in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

This Privacy Policy explains how VeriBuild International collects, stores, protects and processes information supplied through the platform.

Information We Collect

  • Name and contact information
  • Email addresses and phone numbers
  • Business registration information
  • Contractor verification documentation
  • Project-related information and uploaded files
  • Merchant quotations and procurement information
  • Project communications and platform activity records
  • Account profile information

Why We Collect Information

  • To verify users and service providers
  • To manage projects and communications
  • To facilitate escrow and payment processes
  • To maintain platform security
  • To prevent fraud and misuse
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To improve platform performance and functionality

Protection Of Information

VeriBuild International implements reasonable technical, organisational and administrative safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, misuse, loss or alteration.

Your Rights

Users may request access to information held about them, request corrections, update records and request deletion of information where permitted by applicable laws and regulations.

Information Sharing

VeriBuild International does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only where required for platform operations, payment processing, project administration, fraud prevention or compliance with legal obligations.

Terms & Conditions

By accessing, registering for or using VeriBuild International, you agree to comply with these Terms and Conditions.

User Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate and truthful information.
  • Maintain the confidentiality of login credentials.
  • Use the platform lawfully and responsibly.
  • Respect all project participants and platform policies.
  • Avoid submitting misleading or fraudulent information.

Contractor Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate qualifications and documentation.
  • Maintain applicable licences, certifications and compliance records.
  • Complete work according to agreed project requirements.
  • Comply with all applicable laws and industry standards.
  • Act professionally when interacting with homeowners and merchants.

Homeowner Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate project information.
  • Review quotations and documentation carefully.
  • Approve milestone completion honestly and fairly.
  • Comply with agreed payment obligations.
  • Maintain respectful communication with project participants.

Merchant Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate pricing and quotation information.
  • Supply materials as agreed.
  • Maintain lawful business operations.
  • Fulfil delivery commitments and documentation requirements.

Escrow Services

Escrow mechanisms are intended to promote trust between parties. VeriBuild International may impose conditions relating to milestone approvals, verification procedures and platform policies before releasing funds.

Account Suspension

VeriBuild International reserves the right to suspend, restrict or terminate accounts that violate platform policies, submit fraudulent information or engage in unlawful activity.

Changes To Policies

These policies may be updated periodically. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of revised terms and conditions.

Contact Information

For questions regarding privacy, legal matters or platform policies, users may contact VeriBuild International through the official contact channels provided on the platform.
