How VeriBuild Works

VeriBuild provides a structured construction management process that protects homeowners, supports contractors, and ensures merchants receive secure payment through milestone-based project governance.

Welcome back, User
1

Project Submission

The homeowner creates a project and uploads plans, specifications, photos, and project requirements. This information forms the foundation of the project's workflow.

2

Contractor Consultation & Quotation

Verified contractors review project information, conduct consultations where necessary, and submit labour quotations along with material requirements.

3

Merchant Pricing

Merchants receive material requests and submit competitive quotations for the required building materials and delivery services.

4

Project Approval

The homeowner reviews available quotations and selects the preferred contractor and merchant combination before proceeding.

5

Escrow Funding

Project funds are secured within the VeriBuild escrow system. This ensures money is available while protecting all stakeholders from unnecessary risk.

6

Material Procurement

Approved merchants deliver materials according to project requirements. Deliveries are verified and documented within the platform.

7

Milestone Completion

Contractors complete predefined project milestones. Each stage is documented and submitted for review through the platform.

8

Verification & Approval

Homeowners review completed milestones and approve work before any payment release is processed.

9

Automated Payment Release

Once milestones are approved, escrow funds are released according to the agreed payment structure for contractors and merchants.

10

Project Completion & Ratings

Upon completion, all parties can review performance and provide ratings, helping maintain accountability and professional standards.
