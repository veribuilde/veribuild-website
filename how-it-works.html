How VeriBuild Works

VeriBuild combines contractor verification, project governance, merchant participation and protected payments to create a trusted construction ecosystem for South Africa.

Welcome back

1. Create A Project

Project Submission

Homeowners submit project requirements, plans, photographs and supporting information to begin the process.

Contractor Matching

Verified Taskers and Master Contractors review opportunities and prepare quotations based on project scope.

Merchant Participation

Merchants provide quotations for materials and deliveries required for the project.

2. Verify & Approve

Professional Verification

Contractors join through structured vetting processes designed to improve accountability and trust.

Quotation Review

Homeowners compare contractor and merchant quotations before selecting the combination that best fits their project.

Project Approval

Once everyone agrees on pricing and milestones, the project moves forward.

3. Protect The Project

Secure Funding

Project funds are protected through milestone-based payment systems, reducing the risks associated with large deposits and unfinished work.

Material Procurement

Approved merchants provide materials according to project requirements, helping ensure transparency throughout the supply chain.

Progress Tracking

Milestones, documentation and approvals create visibility for everyone involved in the project.

4. Complete & Build Trust

Payment Release

Payments are released after milestones are approved, helping protect homeowners while ensuring professionals are paid fairly.

The Trust Ladder

Successful projects and positive ratings help contractors build stronger reputations and unlock larger opportunities.

A Stronger Industry

VeriBuild brings homeowners, contractors, taskers and merchants together within one trusted ecosystem.

The Two-Tier Model

Tier 1 Verified Taskers

Independent painters, tilers, pavers and handymen start with smaller projects and gradually build their digital reputation.

Tier 2 Master Contractors

Established businesses and registered professionals handle larger and more complex residential projects.

Growth Through Trust

As professionals complete projects successfully, they strengthen their standing and increase their opportunities.
