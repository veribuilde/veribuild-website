How VeriBuild Works

VeriBuild makes it easier and safer for homeowners to connect with trusted contractors and manage construction projects from start to finish.

The VeriBuild Process

01

Post Your Project

Homeowners create a project listing, describe the work needed, upload plans or photos, and provide project details such as location, budget, and timeline.

02

Get Matched With Contractors

Verified contractors in your area are notified about your project and can express interest in providing a quote.

03

Compare Quotes

Review contractor profiles, compare quotes, and evaluate experience, qualifications, and customer reviews before making a decision.

04

Choose Your Contractor

Select the contractor that best suits your budget, timeline, and project requirements.

05

Build With Confidence

Work with a verified contractor and track project progress through the VeriBuild platform.

06

Rate Your Experience

Leave a review and rating to help other homeowners make informed decisions and maintain quality across the platform.
