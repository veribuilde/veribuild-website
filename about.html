About VeriBuild International

VeriBuild International is South Africa's construction trust network. We exist to solve the broken handshake problem that has affected homeowners, contractors, merchants and independent tradespeople for decades. By combining verification, project governance, milestone protection and secure payment systems, we help construction projects move forward with confidence and accountability.

Welcome back

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a safer and more transparent construction industry by protecting project funds, strengthening trust between all parties and giving both established businesses and independent tradespeople access to opportunities they can confidently grow through.

What Makes VeriBuild International Different

Protected Payments

Milestone-based payment systems and escrow protection reduce the risk of disappearing deposits and unfinished projects.

Multi-Level Verification

From independent taskers to fully registered contractors, every professional progresses through a structured trust framework.

Governance & Compliance

VeriBuild International is designed around accountability, documentation and responsible contractor onboarding.

The Two-Tier Trust Network

Tier 1 Verified Taskers

Independent painters, tilers, pavers and handymen can access smaller projects while building a digital reputation and work history.

Tier 2 Master Contractors

Established businesses and registered professionals participate in larger projects supported by deeper verification processes.

The Trust Ladder

Contractors earn higher project limits and stronger reputations through successful project completion and client ratings.

Who We Serve

Homeowners

Homeowners receive greater peace of mind through verified professionals, milestone approvals and protected project funding.

Contractors & Taskers

Construction professionals gain access to opportunities, reputation building and transparent payment systems.

Merchants & Suppliers

Material suppliers benefit from secure project ecosystems that ensure construction budgets reach their intended purpose.

Our Vision

We envision a South African construction industry where trust is no longer based on promises alone. Through verification, secure project governance and transparent financial systems, VeriBuild International aims to become the country's leading trust infrastructure for residential construction and community development.
