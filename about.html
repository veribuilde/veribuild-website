About VeriBuild

VeriBuild was created to bring trust, transparency and accountability to residential construction projects. Through contractor verification, secure escrow management and milestone-based project oversight, we help homeowners, contractors and merchants work together with confidence.

Our Mission

Our mission is to eliminate uncertainty in residential construction by creating a secure digital environment where projects are properly managed, payments are protected, and every stakeholder operates with transparency and accountability.

What Makes VeriBuild Different

Escrow Protection

Project funds remain secure and are only released when approved milestones are completed.

Verified Contractors

Contractors undergo verification processes to help maintain professional standards across the platform.

Project Governance

Every project follows a structured process with milestone tracking and documented approvals.

Who We Serve

Homeowners

Gain visibility into every stage of your project while keeping your funds protected.

Contractors

Access structured projects, transparent milestones and dependable payment processes.

Merchants

Participate in material procurement workflows with secure payment settlement.

Our Vision

We envision a construction industry where trust is built into every transaction. By combining technology, financial governance and project oversight, VeriBuild aims to become the standard platform for secure residential construction projects throughout South Africa.
